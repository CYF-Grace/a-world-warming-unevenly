What have we done so far?

We selected CMIP6 ACCESS-CM2 temperature and precipitation data and made exploratory figures for our proposal. For this prototype, we calculated projected near-surface warming at each model grid cell. We built a map that lets readers choose a location and find places projected to warm by a similar amount. We added a global distribution and a pin so readers can compare their selection with the planet as a whole or with a second place.

What will be the most challenging part to design, and why?

The hardest part will be showing what the selected location has in common with its matches when readers can see several kinds of information at once. Readers need to distinguish similar warming from similar future temperatures, because the selected places can start at very different temperatures. We also need to make the move from this map to our emissions and precipitation comparisons feel connected. We plan to test whether readers can explain what a map match means and what changes when they pin a second place.
