What have we done so far?

We selected CMIP6 ACCESS-CM2 temperature and precipitation data and made exploratory figures for our proposal. For this prototype, we calculated projected near-surface warming and precipitation changes at each model grid cell. We built an interactive map that lets readers switch between temperature and precipitation, choose a location, and find other places projected to experience a similar change in the selected climate variable. We added a global distribution and a pinning feature so readers can compare their selection with the planet as a whole or with a second location.

What will be the most challenging part to design, and why?

The hardest part will be showing what the selected location has in common with its matches when readers can see several kinds of information at once. Readers need to distinguish similar changes in temperature or precipitation from similar future future temperatures or precipitations, because the selected places can start at very different initial conditions. We also need to make the move from warming and precipitations to other variables feel more connected through a narrative. We plan to test whether readers can explain what a map match means and what changes when they pin a second place.
